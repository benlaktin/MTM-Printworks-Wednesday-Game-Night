MTM Printworks Open Games Night

MTM Printworks has open games night by sign up almost every Wednesday Night! Tables are free to book and game mats and terrain are available for use. Games may start anytime after 4:30 PM but must start by 7:00 PM. Last turn is called at 9:45 PM and gaming ends at 10:00 PM. If there are no sign ups, the store closes at its regular 6:00 PM time.

Choose a Wednesday evening gaming session and reserve your table.

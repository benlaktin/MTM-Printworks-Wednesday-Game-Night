MTM Printworks Open Games Night

MTM Printworks has open games night by sign up almost every Wednesday Night. Tables are free to book and game mats and terrain are available for use. Games can begin after 4:30 PM and must start by 7:00 PM. Last turn is called at 9:45 PM and gaming ends at 10:00 PM.
